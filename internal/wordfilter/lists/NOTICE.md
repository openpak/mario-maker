"List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words" (LDNOOBW),
https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words,
commit 5faf2ba42d7b1c0977169ec3611df25a3c08eb13 (fetched 2026-11-01).

`x.txt` is OpenPak's own list (hate figures, groups and slogans, slurs and variants, sexual terms; English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian), AGPL-3.0-only like the rest of the repository. It was checked against the 333,830 archived course titles and 109,654 maker names so that clean titles are not caught.
